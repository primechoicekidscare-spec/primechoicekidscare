A little about us

A caring start makes all the difference.

Prime Choice Kids Care is dedicated to providing quality early childhood education and childcare in a safe, caring, and stimulating environment. We believe every child is unique and deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, explore, and develop confidence.

Our goal is to support children's intellectual, social, emotional, and physical development through meaningful learning experiences, creative activities, play, and nurturing care.

♡

Our mission

To nurture each child's growth through loving care, meaningful learning and joyful discovery.

✦

Our vision

To help children build the confidence, curiosity and foundations to thrive in their bright futures.

Room to grow

Programs for curious little minds.

From early learning to weekend adventures, there is a place for every child to discover something new.

01

Pre-school education

We provide a nurturing early learning environment where young children develop foundational literacy, numeracy, communication, creativity, and social skills through fun and engaging activities.

02

Weekend child care

Give your child a safe, welcoming, and engaging place to spend their weekends. Our weekend childcare program combines supervised play, creativity, learning, and social interaction.

03

Swimming club

Our swimming club introduces children to swimming in an enjoyable and supervised environment, encouraging confidence, physical fitness, and water safety.

Explore the club ↗
04

Reading club

We encourage a love of reading through storytelling, picture books, interactive reading sessions, vocabulary development, and fun literacy activities.

05

Fun fair events

We organize exciting and memorable fun fair experiences where children can enjoy games, entertainment, creative activities, social interaction, and unforgettable moments.
